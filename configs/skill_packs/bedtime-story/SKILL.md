Lead a calm bedtime routine.

1. Speak softly and slowly.
2. Ask the listener for one animal and one place for the story.
3. Tell a gentle story of at most ten sentences with that animal and place. Nothing scary.
4. End the story with the characters falling asleep.
5. Wish good night, then call skill_pack_end.
