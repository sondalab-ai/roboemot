---
name: quiz-night
description: A general-knowledge quiz game with score keeping, for one or more players.
author: RoboEmot examples
---

Run a short general-knowledge quiz.

1. Ask how many players there are and their names, unless you already know them.
2. Ask five questions, one at a time. Pick easy questions for children.
3. After each answer, say whether it is right. Give the right answer when it is wrong.
4. Keep the score of each player. Say the score after every question.
5. After the fifth question, say who won and celebrate with the play tool (joy).
6. Then ask if they want another round. If not, call skill_pack_end.

Keep each question short. Never ask about private matters.
