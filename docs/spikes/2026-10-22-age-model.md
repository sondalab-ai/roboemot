# Research: a cheaper age-group (and gender) model for the CM4

Date: 2026-10-22. Research only, no code changed. Revision 2: the project owner dropped the
commercial-use requirement (decision 2026-10-22). Non-commercial licences are acceptable; each licence is
recorded, with whether we may host the weights or must fetch them from the original pinned URL at install time.
The ranking under the commercial constraint is kept at the end.

## Question

Today two FairFace ViT-B models (dima806, dynamic INT8, about 87 MB each, 224x224) estimate age group and
gender per face crop (`vision/onnx.py` `FairFaceEstimator`, manifest `vision/models.yaml`). On the robot the
`roboemot-attributes` thread uses about 93% of one core continuously (`robot-cpu-profile-2026-10-22.md`).
Wanted: well under 100 ms per face crop on one Cortex-A72 core (ONNX Runtime CPU), a small file, children
covered, a reliable "under 18 vs adult"; gender is secondary.

Safety metric: **share of minors labelled confident adult**. Baseline: the deployed INT8 ViT, about **12.4%**
on all 10,954 FairFace validation faces (11.3% FP32 + 1.1 points INT8, `docs/spikes/2026-10-m1-models.md`).

## Short answer

- **Usable with no training: AdamCodd `yolo11n-face-age`, for age only.** It is a 10.5 MB YOLO11-nano with
  three classes (0-14, 15-22, 22+). I ran it on all 10,954 FairFace validation faces (method below). At input
  160 and score 0.5 it labels **10.4% of minors as adult**, against the ViT's 12.4%. At the ViT's adult
  acceptance rate (matched point, on the spike's 1,400-face subset) it is 9.5% vs 12.6%. Estimated CM4
  latency is about 50 to 130 ms per crop at 160, about 30 to 80 ms at 128. Licence: CC-BY-NC-4.0.
- Its caveats: it has no gender and no senior class. The minor cutoff moves to 22: its 15-22 class counts as a
  possible minor, so adults aged 20 to 22 keep `minor_present` on. Its training set includes FairFace, and
  nothing says the validation split was held out, so these numbers may be optimistic. The spike raised the same
  doubt about the ViT baseline.
- No small, no-training model gives usable gender. InsightFace `genderage` (1.3 MB) is the only small one,
  and the spike measured 18.5% gender error with 20% withheld. Its age output is unusable: it labelled 85.9% of
  minors as adult.
- Allowing non-commercial data also improves the trained options. UTKFace has exact ages from 0 to 116, so a
  trained model can split at exactly 18 instead of FairFace's 20.

## Facts from the code that constrain the choice

- Groups: `AGE_LABEL_GROUPS` (`vision/onnx.py:59`) maps FairFace 0-2 and 3-9 to child, 10-19 to teen, 20-59 to
  adult, 60+ to senior. `possible_minor` (`registry/records.py:32`) is true unless the group is exactly adult or
  senior, so today's effective cutoff is 20.
- The 0.8 rule (`identity.estimate_confidence_min`, shared by age and gender) turns weak estimates into
  `unknown`, which counts as a possible minor. A weak model fails safe. The unsafe error is a confident adult
  label on a minor; the cost of a cautious model is adults kept as possible minors.
- Per track: `vision.attribute_crops` crops (default 3), at least `identity.estimate_frames_min` (validator at
  `config/schema.py:3442`). Crops are the detector box squared plus `vision.attribute_crop_margin` (0.25 per
  side, `pipeline.py` `_crop`). FairFace's "0.25 padding" images are a close match to that crop.
- Gender is used for forms of address; it changes register parameters only if
  `gender_affects_params` is true (`config/schema.py:358`, default false). An `unknown` gender is allowed.
- SFace: input 1x3x112x112, output 128-d (checked locally). Computed anyway in the identity burst for faces of
  at least `vision.embed_min_face_px`.

## Latency method

Nothing here was measured on the CM4. Two estimates, both given as ranges:

1. Mac M3 Pro, ONNX Runtime 1.31, **1 thread** (the attribute thread uses 1 intra-op thread), x 8 to 20 (the
   spike's assumed CM4 slowdown).
2. MAC count / throughput: about 2 to 5 GMAC/s on one A72 core for dense layers, 1 to 2.5 GMAC/s for
   depthwise-heavy networks, plus 1 to 2 ms per call.

The A72 is ARMv8.0-A: no FP16 arithmetic and no SDOT. FP16 files shrink but do not run faster on ORT CPU.
INT8 gains less than on the M3.

Today: INT8 pair 266 ms on the Mac (1 thread) x 8 to 20 = 2.1 to 5.3 s per crop, x 3 crops = **about 6.4 to
16 s per new track**.

## Measured: yolo11n-face-age on FairFace validation

Method: each FairFace 0.25-padding validation image (one face) is letterboxed to S x S, grey 114 padding, RGB,
/255. Of the anchors, the one with the highest class score is kept. Its class is the prediction and its raw
sigmoid score is the confidence. That is the value the integration would pass to `AttributeSample`.
"Adult" means class 22+ with a score of at least t. A score under t counts as unknown, which is a possible
minor. Minors are FairFace bins 0-19 (n = 2,736) and adults are bins 20+. Timing is the network only, 1 thread.
The scripts are scratch files (`eval_yolo_age.py`, `matched.py`) in this session's scratchpad and were not
added to `scripts/bench/`.

Full validation set, n = 10,954:

| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 320 | 22.2 | 180 to 440 | 0.5 | 5.4% | 10.9% | 1.2% | 72.5% | 50.4% | 4.8% |
| 224 | 11.9 | 95 to 240 | 0.5 | 7.4% | 15.7% | 1.1% | 77.5% | 59.2% | 5.7% |
| 160 | 6.4 | 52 to 130 | 0.5 | 10.4% | 21.1% | 2.3% | 82.3% | 68.0% | 9.2% |
| 128 | 4.0 | 32 to 80 | 0.5 | 11.3% | 22.3% | 3.0% | 79.5% | 64.5% | 13.8% |

Minors labelled adult at 160, t = 0.5, by race: East Asian 10.4%, Indian 9.3%, Black 8.5%, White 10.6%,
Middle Eastern 9.5%, Latino_Hispanic 14.3%, Southeast Asian 10.0%. For comparison, the ViT with the 0.8 rule
ranged from 5.6% (Black) to 16.2% (White).

Matched operating point, on the spike's 1,400-face subset (357 minors), where the per-image INT8 ViT
probabilities are saved (`scripts/bench/results/fairface_int8_spike_int8.npz`):

| Model | Threshold | Adults accepted | Minors as adult | 10-19 as adult |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FairFace ViT INT8 (shipped, 0.8 rule) | 0.8 | 83.5% | 12.6% | 27.7% |
| yolo11n 160 | 0.455 (matched) | 83.5% | 9.5% | 19.4% |
| yolo11n 128 | 0.322 (matched) | 83.5% | 12.9% | 25.2% |
| yolo11n 224 | 0 (cannot reach 83.5%; max 79.1%) | 79.1% | 7.6% | 15.5% |

How to read this:

- At the same adult acceptance, the 160 input leaks fewer minors than the shipped ViT: 9.5% vs 12.6%. With
  357 minors the 95% interval is about ±3.4 points, so this is "at least as good", not "proven better". The
  128 input is on par with the ViT.
- **Contamination, which cannot be checked:** the model card lists FairFace among its training sources, and the
  validation split may be in it. The 0-14 bucket also lines up with FairFace's bins. The ViT baseline has the same
  doubt. No age-labelled dataset available locally is outside both training sets. The only clean test is on the
  robot, with consenting people of known age.
- The 0.8 rule is too strict for this model's raw scores, which are independent sigmoids, not a softmax. At 0.8
  it leaks only about 2% of minors but accepts only 51 to 61% of adults (27 to 37% of people in their 20s). The
  threshold should be about 0.5, either as its own setting or by rescaling the score.
- Adults aged 20 to 22 fall into its 15-22 class by design, which shows up as the lower 20-29 acceptance. That is
  the cost of the cutoff at 22.
- At runtime, the median over 3 crops should lower both error rates. That was not measured, because FairFace has
  one image per face.

## Candidates, re-ranked

"Redistribute?" says whether we may host and pin the file ourselves. If not, it is fetched from the original
pinned URL at install time, which `roboemot models fetch` already does for the current models.

### 1. AdamCodd yolo11n-face-age on the face crop (no training, age only)

- Source: https://huggingface.co/AdamCodd/yolo11n-face-age, revision `410bdd84fa762b6aa550ef73bd57865a2d30be37`,
  file `model.onnx` (10,513,214 bytes, sha256
  `e37945afcbc1b855f4a66bbf1e13db9d19d5ca2e8ddb2ffe2be2906d53f5ff93`, verified locally). There is also
  `model_fp16.onnx` (5.4 MB), which brings no speed-up on the A72.
- Licence: the model card says CC-BY-NC-4.0, "strictly for non-commercial research". The ONNX metadata
  says `AGPL-3.0` (the Ultralytics default stamp). Both are recorded. The card also says it should not be used
  for critical age verification.
- Redistribute? CC-BY-NC allows non-commercial redistribution with attribution, and AGPL-3.0 allows it with
  source. Fetching from the pinned Hugging Face URL avoids both questions, so fetch is preferred.
- Architecture and input: YOLO11-nano detector, dynamic input (exported `dynamic: True`, opset 12, no NMS).
  Output is 1 x 7 x anchors: box plus 3 class scores. It is used here as a classifier on the padded face crop,
  which matches its training data of face-centred images.
- Published accuracy (model card, own test set): 0-14 class 91%, 15-22 class 74%, 22+ class 82%; 1% of
  children predicted 22+. For the FairFace measurement, see above.
- Trained on about 75,000 curated faces from IMDB-WIKI, Adience, UTKFace and FairFace (model card).
- Weaknesses: no gender, no senior class, cutoff at 22. The 15-22 bucket is the weak one (74% on its own test).
  The card notes it may fail on AI-generated or cartoon faces.
- CM4 latency: about 52 to 130 ms at 160, about 32 to 80 ms at 128 (Mac x 8 to 20). By MAC count, YOLO11n is
  6.5 GFLOPs at 640 (about 3.2 GMAC), so about 0.2 GMAC at 160 and about 0.13 at 128, which gives 40 to 100 ms
  and 26 to 65 ms. Per track (3 crops at 160): about 0.15 to 0.4 s, against 6.4 to 16 s today.
- Integration notes: decode is about 10 lines (letterbox, take the best anchor). It needs its own confidence
  threshold (about 0.5) or a rescaled score. Map 0-14 to child, 15-22 to teen and 22+ to adult. Senior is lost,
  which matters only to the affect register.

### 2. MobileNetV3-Small multi-task (age + gender), trained by us on FairFace + UTKFace

- Source: `timm/mobilenetv3_small_100.lamb_in1k`, https://huggingface.co/timm/mobilenetv3_small_100.lamb_in1k
  (revision `1824797e7887cbec1990e4adbd6675960a36c589`, Apache-2.0). Data: FairFace (CC BY 4.0) and UTKFace
  (non-commercial research only).
- Redistribute? The weights would be ours. UTKFace's terms say nothing explicit about trained weights, so the
  result is non-commercial at most. We can host it.
- What changes now that non-commercial data is allowed: UTKFace's exact ages allow a true 18 cutoff and a
  senior class. Its labels were estimated with DEX and checked by a human annotator (project page), so they are
  noisy near 18. A heavy NC teacher (for example faceage DINOv3 below) could pseudo-label FairFace with exact
  ages.
- Architecture: about 2.5 M params, 128 or 160 px input, age-group and gender heads, about 4 to 10 MB FP32.
- Accuracy: none until trained. MobileAgeNet (MobileNetV3-Large, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17007) reports
  MAE 4.65 on UTKFace; its weights are not released.
- CM4 latency estimate: about 8 to 20 ms at 128, 13 to 30 ms at 160.
- Effort: a training script, the FairFace eval harness above, YuNet-recropped training images to match the robot.

### 3. SFace embedding + tiny head (gender first, age optional)

- Source: SFace from opencv_zoo, commit `48b10fa505aeb51fbd127fc60a5c660040e823b7`, Apache-2.0, already in the
  manifest. The head is ours: logistic regression or a small MLP, a few KB.
- Marginal cost about zero, because the embedding is already computed.
- Best use under the new constraint: the gender head, so that option 1 can ship without the gender ViT. Age from
  an identity embedding is a structural risk: recognition losses suppress age, and recognition training sets
  contain few children.
- Data: FairFace (and UTKFace) passed through YuNet, `align_face` and SFace, as on the robot. Hours of work,
  minutes of fitting. Accuracy is unknown until fitted. Terhörst et al. 2020
  (https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.09918) show gender and age are recoverable from FaceNet and ArcFace templates.

### 4. FairFace ViT-B age only, 1 crop (no training, stopgap)

- Source and licence unchanged: https://huggingface.co/dima806/fairface_age_image_detection (revision
  `4e02ab8057ea7fd74b1670940995c5dfda3e6ec0`, Apache-2.0), ONNX from onnx-community (revision
  `fd04cddf04982f6acc1ac76fb1a31b44c9285596`).
- Cost: about 1.1 to 2.7 s per track (Mac 135 ms x 8 to 20), about 6x less than today. It needs
  `estimate_frames_min` lowered to 1.
- Known accuracy: 12.4% minors as adult. It is superseded by option 1 unless the robot measurement of option 1
  disappoints.

## Ranking

1. **yolo11n-face-age at 160 (or 128), age only, no training.** Measured on FairFace: 10.4% of minors labelled
   adult at t = 0.5, against the ViT's 12.4%; at matched adult acceptance, 9.5% against 12.6%. CM4 estimate 52 to
   130 ms (128: 32 to 80 ms). 10.5 MB. CC-BY-NC-4.0, fetched from the pinned URL. Possible FairFace contamination;
   cutoff 22; no gender or senior class.
2. **MobileNetV3-Small multi-task trained on FairFace + UTKFace.** About 8 to 30 ms (estimate), 4 to 10 MB,
   exact 18 cutoff, includes gender. Needs training; accuracy unknown until then.
3. **SFace + logistic head.** About 0 ms extra and a few KB. The cheapest way to recover gender alongside option 1.
   Needs a small fit; age from it is risky.
4. **FairFace ViT-B age only, 1 crop.** No training; about 1.1 to 2.7 s per track; known 12.4% leak. Superseded
   by option 1.
5. **Heavy references** (UTKFace ViT-B, Civitai minor/adult ViT-B, MiVOLO v2, faceage DINOv3). All too slow;
   useful only as teachers or as accuracy references.

Not usable: InsightFace `genderage` (85.9% of minors labelled adult), OpenVINO retail-0013 (no children).

## Recommendation

Replace the age ViT with **yolo11n-face-age on the existing padded crop at 160 px**, with its own score threshold
of 0.5, mapping 15-22 to teen (a possible minor). Fetch it from the pinned Hugging Face URL. Before switching,
measure it on the robot with `scripts/bench/run_cm4.py`. If 160 is over about 100 ms, use 128, which is on par
with the ViT on the safety metric. For gender, fit an SFace logistic head (option 3). Until that exists, leave
gender `unknown` (neutral forms of address) or keep the gender ViT on 1 crop.

Trade-offs, plainly:

- This is the only no-training option, and it covers age only.
- Its safety number may be flattered by FairFace being in its training data. The robot check with known-age
  volunteers is the real test. The baseline it beats carries the same doubt.
- People aged 20 to 22 stay possible minors, and at t = 0.5 about a third of people in their 20s are not
  confirmed adult per crop. That is more often `minor_present` than today, which is safe but more restrictive.
- The senior group disappears unless a later model (option 2) brings it back.
- The licence is non-commercial (CC-BY-NC, plus AGPL stamped in the ONNX metadata). That fits the 2026-10-22
  decision; a later commercial use would need option 2 trained on FairFace alone (see below).
- If the cutoff at 22 or the missing senior class becomes a problem, option 2 is the follow-up. It trains on
  UTKFace's exact ages, so it can split at 18 and keep gender in the same model.
- Track churn (jobs re-triggered by new tracks) is a separate load problem and still worth fixing. At about
  0.15 to 0.4 s per track it matters much less.

## Previous ranking under the commercial constraint (revision 1, superseded)

Under "commercial use and redistribution required", no ready-made small model qualified: InsightFace, UTKFace-,
CelebA- and CC-BY-NC-trained models were excluded on licence, and OpenVINO retail-0013 on coverage.
The ranking was:

1. MobileNetV3-Small multi-task trained on FairFace only (cutoff 20).
2. SFace + head.
3. FairFace ViT age only, 1 crop.
4. MobileNetV3-Large.
5. FairFace ResNet-34 (repository without a licence).

The recommendation was to train option 1, with the SFace head as a one-day pre-check. That path is still the one
to take if commercial use comes back.
